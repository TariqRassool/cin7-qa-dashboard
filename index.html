Sampling & selection criteria
How tickets are sampled
Each agent's monthly solved and closed tickets are sampled at approximately 11% of monthly volume, with a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 8 tickets per month. Tickets with no agent replies are excluded.

Tickets are prioritised in the following order:
  1. Bad CSAT (rating below 5)
  2. Slow FRT (>24 hrs) + High Complexity (8+ replies)
  3. Slow FRT alone
  4. High Complexity alone
  5. Medium Complexity (4–7 replies)
  6. Short tickets (1–3 replies)
Scoring framework
Two scorecard versions are in use depending on ticket date:
Pre-20 May 2026 — 35 points
7 categories × 5 pts · Pass threshold: 32/35 (90%)
Post-20 May 2026 — 55 points
7 base + 4 MFR categories × 5 pts · Pass: 50/55 (90%)

Green — ≥ 90%
